Borrow a hand from your past self.

The puzzle

There are two pads, A and B, and one locked gate. You have 8 beats per route. Move to a pad, then Record echo. That route replays alongside your next attempt. Record a second echo for the other pad, then slip through the gate to the star.

An echo stays at the last square of its recorded route. Only your two most recent echoes remain. Bumping a wall still uses a beat.
