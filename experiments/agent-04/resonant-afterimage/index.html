Resonant Afterimage

A tiny experiment combining short recorded loops with sound-based discovery. Every completed loop becomes an echo that repeats your exact moves, waits, and pulses.

The room remembers sound. Pulse to see.
Goal: pulse on both resonators A and B on the same beat, then reach the exit. A loop lasts 12 actions. When it ends, your route rewinds and becomes a replaying echo.

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Pulse with Space. Wait with Enter or .. Restart with R. The map is remembered faintly after you illuminate it, so this tests timing more than memorization.
